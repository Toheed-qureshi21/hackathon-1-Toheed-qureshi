## Brand & Style

This design system targets affluent globetrotters, points-and-miles optimizers, and global executives managing multi-program loyalty equity. The aesthetic balances the precision of high-frequency fintech terminals with the luxury of modern aviation first-class suites. 

### Design Direction
The interface employs an **Atmospheric Minimalist & Glass-Precision** aesthetic:
- **Depth through Stratification:** Pure deep-space navy canvases host razor-sharp, translucent surface cards with sub-pixel structural borders.
- **Instrument-Grade Information Hierarchy:** Data density is celebrated through mono-spaced metric blocks, structured itineraries, and clear yield indicators.
- **Intentional Electric Sparks:** The interface remains largely disciplined, dark, and muted, punctuated deliberately by high-energy electric lime and violet highlights to signal value-creation and AI intelligence.
- **Restraint and Luxury:** Interactions feel weighted, frictionless, and fluid, avoiding gratuitous neon glows in favor of surgical clarity and sharp architectural framing.

## Colors

The palette leverages a dark-first hierarchy engineered for prolonged visual comfort during complex flight routing and redemption calculations.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas / Base (`#080B14`):** Midnight void. The deepest spatial layer.
- **Panel / Structural Shell (`#101625`):** Side navigation, app bars, and pinned analytics drawers.
- **Card Surface (`#171F31`):** Flight cards, loyalty portfolio modules, dynamic price charts.
- **Elevated Overlay (`#1E273E`):** Dropdowns, tooltips, flyouts, and active interactive cards.
- **Border / Structural Divider (`#273149`):** 1px boundaries defining panels and cards without high visual friction.
- **Border Active / Subtle Focus (`#3B4968`):** Hover states and selected boundary rings.

### Typography & Readability
- **Primary Text (`#F4F7FC`):** Display headings, high-priority flight numbers, conversion rates, and values.
- **Muted Text (`#9AA7BD`):** Labels, departure times, metadata, table column headers.
- **Subtle / Ghost Text (`#5E6D88`):** Disabled values, placeholder fields, watermarks.

### Functional Accents
- **Primary / Electric Lime (`#C7F36B`):** The primary brand hook representing liquidity, point redemption yield, and highest-tier actions. Paired strictly with dark text (`#080B14`) when applied to solid surfaces.
- **Secondary / Aviation Blue (`#7EA7FF`):** Interactive links, route trajectories, status tracking, and neutral financial data.
- **Tertiary / Violet AI (`#A78BFA`):** Autonomous AI flight search, redemption algorithm triggers, and predictive point transfers.
- **Success / Mint (`#72E6C1`):** Verified transfers, seat confirm approvals, positive point valuations.
- **Danger / Alert (`#FF7C86`):** Blackout dates, expired points, cancellation warnings, redemption drops.

## Typography

Typography establishes an authoritative tension between **Space Grotesk**’s engineered, aerodynamic geometry and **Inter**’s neutral, legible precision.

- **Headlines & Display:** Space Grotesk commands attention for airport IATA codes (e.g., JFK ➔ HND), points balance totals, and hero redemption values. Its tech-forward character reinforces analytical rigour.
- **Body & Data:** Inter provides friction-free legibility across complex multi-carrier fare rules, transfer partner conversion rates, and redemption matrices.
- **Labels & Micro-metrics:** Set in Space Grotesk uppercase with tracked letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to mimic radar navigation consoles and trading readouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by structural horizontal rules and vertical rails.

### Structure & Margins
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, `2rem` (32px) outer page margins, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Left side-navigation dock (`280px` fixed).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid, `1.5rem` (24px) margins, `1rem` (16px) gutters. Side navigation collapses to an icon dock (`72px`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `1rem` (16px) margins, `0.75rem` (12px) gutters. Primary controls anchor to a sticky floating bottom utility dock.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Internal Component Padding:** 
  - Micro-chips & badges: `space-xs` (4px) vertical, `space-sm` (8px) horizontal.
  - Form fields & action buttons: `space-sm` (8px) to `space-md` (16px) vertical, `space-lg` (24px) horizontal.
  - Flight cards & portfolio summary modules: `space-lg` (24px) internal padding.
- **Section & Layout Spacing:** Use `space-xl` (32px) between closely paired cards and `space-2xl` (48px) to `space-3xl` (64px) between disparate workflow steps.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain the ultra-premium dark aesthetic, this design system eschews muddy, high-spread black drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Stratification and Luminescent Edge Lit Borders**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#080B14`. Non-elevated, absorbs background luminescence.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Navy Panels):** `#101625` with a subtle 1px border of `#273149`. Zero blur elevation.
3. **Level 2 (Active Cards & Flight Itineraries):** `#171F31` background, 1px `#273149` boundary. On hover: 1px border transitions to `#3B4968` accompanied by an ambient, tinted glow: `0 8px 32px -8px rgba(126, 167, 255, 0.08)`.
4. **Level 3 (AI Insights & Optimization Highlights):** `#171F31` base infused with an intentional directional top highlight: a 1px border gradient transitioning from `#A78BFA` to transparent, plus ambient aura: `0 12px 40px -12px rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.12)`.
5. **Level 4 (Modals, Flight Booking Confirmations & Drawers):** `#101625` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, enclosed within a crisp 1px `#273149` border, supported by deep structural elevation: `0 24px 64px -16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an intentional, engineered silhouette with restrained corner radii (`roundedness: 1` — Soft, 4px base radius). 

### Corner Geometry
- **Micro UI (Badges, Checkboxes, Route Tags):** `4px` (`rounded-sm`).
- **Input Fields & Buttons:** `6px` to `8px` (`rounded-md`).
- **Cards, Panels & Flight Itinerary Blocks:** `8px` to `12px` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Pills (Strictly Reserved):** Status pills (e.g., "Non-Stop", "First Class", "AI Re-route") leverage 9999px pill shapes to clearly contrast against architectural card frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Redemption / Transfer Action):** Background `#C7F36B`, text `#080B14`, font Space Grotesk Bold. Sharp 6px radius. Hover: subtle scale and electric glow `0 0 20px rgba(199, 243, 107, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary (Aviation / Standard Interactive):** Background `#171F31`, border 1px `#273149`, text `#F4F7FC`. Hover: border `#7EA7FF`, text `#7EA7FF`.
- **AI Algorithmic Action:** Background `#171F31`, border 1px `#A78BFA`, text `#A78BFA`. Features a miniature pulsing spark indicator. Hover: background `rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.12)`.
- **Ghost:** Text `#9AA7BD`, transparent background. Hover: text `#F4F7FC`, background `#101625`.

### Flight & Reward Cards
- Surfaces are built with `#171F31` and outlined with `#273149`.
- **The Split Layout:** Left side displays segment times, airline alliance badges, layovers, and aircraft specs (Inter Regular & Medium); Right side contains the points-valuation module highlighting "Cents Per Point" (CPP) yield in `#C7F36B` and direct cash comparisons in `#9AA7BD`.

### Chips & Route Tags
- **Alliance & Cabin Badges:** Background `#101625`, 1px border `#273149`, text `#9AA7BD`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Optimal Deal Badge:** Background `rgba(199, 243, 107, 0.12)`, border 1px `#C7F36B`, text `#C7F36B`.

### Form & Input Fields
- **Search Inputs (Airport, Date, Points Budget):** Height 48px, background `#101625`, border 1px `#273149`, typography Inter 14px `#F4F7FC`.
- **Focus State:** 1px border `#7EA7FF` with `0 0 0 1px #7EA7FF`. No standard browser focus rings.
- **Airport IATA Input:** Features bolded `Space Grotesk` uppercase text with micro city label placed directly beneath.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px squares with 3px corners. Border 1px `#273149`. Checked state: Background `#C7F36B`, check icon `#080B14`.
- **Radio Buttons:** 18x18px concentric circle. Unchecked: `#273149`. Checked: Border `#C7F36B`, inner dot `#C7F36B` with dark gap.

### AI Co-Pilot Redemption Drawer
- Pinned conversational optimization strip: `#101625` surface, separated by a 1px border with a faint ambient `#A78BFA` radial backlight.
- Displays dynamic prompt chips: *"Find Qsuite via British Airways Avios"*, *"Alert me if Singapore Suites opens on Nov 14"*.
